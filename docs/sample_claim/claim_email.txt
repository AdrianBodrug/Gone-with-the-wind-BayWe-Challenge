Subject: Business interruption notification – Wind Farm T (demo) – outage 01.09.2026 to 03.09.2026

Dear Demo Insurance AG claims team,

We are writing to notify you of a business interruption at Wind Farm T (demo), insured under policy DEMO-0000-00.

WHAT HAPPENED
On 01 September 2026 at 00:00, the communication connection to the wind farm was lost and remained unavailable until 04 September 2026 at 00:00 (local time, Europe/Berlin), a duration of 72 hours. A production stop is suspected; possible causes include cable damage between the wind farm and the grid connection point or a technical fault preventing feed-in. The root cause is under investigation.

Affected turbines: T1, T2, T3 (4.5 MW rated power each).

ESTIMATED ENERGY YIELD LOSS
Because the turbines' own wind measurements are unavailable for this period, we reconstructed the wind conditions from reference wind farms nearby and applied the turbines' power curves.

  Estimated lost energy, wind farm total (P50):  312.0 MWh
  Likely range (P10 – P90):                       260.8 – 351.3 MWh

VALUE OF THE LOST ENERGY
Depending on how the wind farm's electricity is remunerated, the lost revenue is approximately:

  Day-ahead market price:                    EUR 41,938  (range EUR 35,059 – 47,225)
  EEG (market price + 0.00 EUR/MWh premium): EUR 41,938  (range EUR 35,059 – 47,225)
  PPA, fixed price (73.13 EUR/MWh):          EUR 22,814  (range EUR 19,072 – 25,691)

- EEG premium = max(0, reference value 73.30 EUR/MWh − monthly market value of onshore wind (2026-09: 109.40 EUR/MWh, German average, not the price of this outage)); the market value was above the reference value, so no premium was due and EEG equals the day-ahead value.
- Market benchmarks used where the contract price was not provided: EEG reference value 73.30 EUR/MWh, PPA price 73.13 EUR/MWh – to be replaced by the actual contract values.

METHOD AND ACCURACY
For each affected turbine, the wind speed during the outage was reconstructed as a weighted sum of wind speeds measured at reference turbines in other wind farms (H1, H2, H5, O2). Each reference is first shifted by the time delay that best matches the target turbine and smoothed over 30 to 130 minutes (chosen per turbine); all weights are forced to be non-negative. The smoothing, the references and the extra terms (wind-direction correction curve, air pressure, time-of-day dependent slope, 3-hour temperature change, turbulence intensity, turbulence) were selected step by step on the organisers' shown test week (4-9 Sep) with the model fitted without that week, and kept only where they lowered the wind and power errors there. Lost energy is the turbine power curve averaged over the model's historical error distribution.
Tested on periods where the actual measurements are known, the method reconstructed wind speed with a typical error of 1.35 m/s (RMSE, on the 04.09.–09.09.2026 validation week, which was held out from the model entirely). Over 29 historical 3-day blocks the model was not fitted on, the energy deviated from the measured value by ±11% on average. Full details, charts and the input-data fingerprints are in the attached report.
