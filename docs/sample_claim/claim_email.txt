Subject: Business interruption notification – Wind Farm T (demo) – outage 01.09.2026 to 03.09.2026

Dear Demo Insurance AG claims team,

We are writing to notify you of a business interruption at Wind Farm T (demo), insured under policy DEMO-0000-00.

WHAT HAPPENED
On 01 September 2026 at 00:00, the communication connection to the wind farm was lost and remained unavailable until 04 September 2026 at 00:00 (local time, Europe/Berlin), a duration of 72 hours. A production stop is suspected; possible causes include cable damage between the wind farm and the grid connection point or a technical fault preventing feed-in. The root cause is under investigation.

Affected turbines: T1, T2, T3 (4.5 MW class).

ESTIMATED ENERGY YIELD LOSS
Because the turbines' own wind measurements are unavailable for this period, we reconstructed the wind conditions from reference wind farms nearby and applied the turbines' power curves.

  Estimated lost energy (best estimate, P50):  313.6 MWh
  Likely range (P10 – P90):                    273.1 – 368.1 MWh
    T1: 89.2 MWh (range 72.2 – 104.4 MWh)
    T2: 126.2 MWh (range 116.5 – 149.6 MWh)
    T3: 98.2 MWh (range 84.4 – 114.1 MWh)

Energy lost beyond the 24-hour waiting period: 191.1 MWh (P50).

Valued at 73.50 EUR/MWh (demo value), this corresponds to approximately EUR 23,046 (range EUR 20,075 – 27,057), or EUR 14,043 after the waiting period.

METHOD AND ACCURACY
Wind speeds at the affected turbines were reconstructed from 7 reference turbines in park(s) H, O outside the affected wind farm. A linear regression, fitted on all available history outside the outage, relates each target turbine's wind speed to the park-average reference wind speed (including values up to 30 minutes before and after each interval) and the reference wind direction. Lost energy is the turbine power curve applied to the reconstructed wind, averaged over the model's historical error distribution.
On historical periods where actual measurements are known, the method reconstructed wind speed with a typical error of 1.15 m/s (RMSE, 27 test windows of 3 days) and the energy over a 3-day window with a typical deviation of ±10%. Full details, charts and the input-data fingerprints are in the attached report.
