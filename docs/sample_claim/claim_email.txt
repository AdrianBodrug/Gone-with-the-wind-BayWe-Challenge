Subject: Business interruption notification – Wind Farm T (demo) – outage 01.09.2026 to 03.09.2026

Dear Demo Insurance AG claims team,

We are writing to notify you of a business interruption at Wind Farm T (demo), insured under policy DEMO-0000-00.

WHAT HAPPENED
On 01 September 2026 at 00:00, the communication connection to the wind farm was lost and remained unavailable until 04 September 2026 at 00:00 (local time, Europe/Berlin), a duration of 72 hours. A production stop is suspected; possible causes include cable damage between the wind farm and the grid connection point or a technical fault preventing feed-in. The root cause is under investigation.

Affected turbines: T1, T2, T3 (4.5 MW rated power each).

ESTIMATED ENERGY YIELD LOSS
Because the turbines' own wind measurements are unavailable for this period, we reconstructed the wind conditions from reference wind farms nearby and applied the turbines' power curves.

  Estimated lost energy, wind farm total (P50):  311.0 MWh
  Likely range (P10 – P90):                       272.7 – 355.5 MWh

VALUE OF THE LOST ENERGY
Depending on how the wind farm's electricity is remunerated, the lost revenue is approximately:

  Day-ahead market price:                             EUR 41,539  (range EUR 36,418 – 47,474)
  EEG market premium (reference value 73.30 EUR/MWh): EUR 41,539  (range EUR 36,418 – 47,474)
  PPA, fixed price (73.13 EUR/MWh):                   EUR 22,745  (range EUR 19,942 – 25,995)

- EEG: market price plus a premium of max(0, reference value − monthly market value of onshore wind (2026-09: 109.40 EUR/MWh)); no premium was due in this period, so EEG equals the market value.
- Market benchmarks used where the contract price was not provided: EEG reference value 73.30 EUR/MWh, PPA price 73.13 EUR/MWh – to be replaced by the actual contract values.

METHOD AND ACCURACY
For each affected turbine, the wind speed during the outage was reconstructed as a weighted sum of wind speeds measured at reference turbines in other wind farms (H1, H2, H3, H4, H5, O1, O2). Each reference is first shifted by the time delay that best matches the target turbine and smoothed over 130 minutes; all weights are forced to be non-negative. References and extra terms (wind-direction correction curve, wind direction, turbulence) were kept only where they improved accuracy in cross-validation over 3-day blocks of history. Lost energy is the turbine power curve averaged over the model's historical error distribution.
On historical periods where actual measurements are known, the method reconstructed wind speed with a typical error of 1.14 m/s (RMSE, cross-validated on 29 historical 3-day blocks) and the energy over a 3-day window with a typical deviation of ±9%. Full details, charts and the input-data fingerprints are in the attached report.
